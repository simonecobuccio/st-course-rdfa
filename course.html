Course Description

This course is part of the Master BADS taught at Polimi GSoM, the Politecnico di Milano Business School.
Students will learn about the logic behind RDF and OWL, learning the use of SPARQL and how to apply these concepts by using Ontop in the field of big data.

General Information

This course is delivered in Milan, Italy, in-person only. However, students may watch the recordings that will be made available online afterwards.
The professor is Matteo Belcao. He can be contacted at matteo.belcao@quantiaconsulting.com throughout the course.
The whole course is taught in English. The number of credits for this course is 2.

Course Structure

This course is composed of three classes: "Introduction to the 'variety dimension' of Big Data - The RDF data model", "Publishing smart data using RDF", and "OWL ontological language - Ontology-based data integration", for a total of approximately 15 hours. The first class is taught on 13 May 2026, the second on 18 May 2026, and the last on 19 May 2026. The exam will take place on 28 May 2026, starting at 1 PM.

The course material consists of six slideshows, each corresponding to a specific topic:

  • Smart Data and Smart Machines
  • RDF Data Model
  • Querying the Semantic Layer with SPARQL
  • RDFa Syntax for the Web
  • Conceptual Modeling with RDFS and OWL
  • Ontology-Based Data Access Using Ontop
